Parental Control Policy

Version 1.0 — Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how a parent or guardian can link their HelpNaud account to a teen’s account, what they can adjust, what they can never see, and how accounts are unlinked.

Overview

Parental control lets a parent or legal guardian link their HelpNaud account to a teen’s account to adjust certain features, set time windows and receive safety notifications in limited situations.

Linking accounts never gives access to the teen’s conversations, chat history or real-time activity. Either the teen or the parent or guardian can unlink the accounts at any time.

A parent or guardian can link several teens. A teen can be linked to only one parent or guardian at a time.

These features are being rolled out gradually. Those not yet available for your account appear as “coming soon” in Settings.

Linking accounts

The invitation can be started by the parent or guardian, or by the teen.

Inviting a teen

  1. Open Settings from your profile menu.
  2. Choose Parental control, then Add a family member.
  3. Enter the teen’s email address or phone number.
  4. Send the invitation.
The teen receives a link to accept. Once accepted, their name appears under Family members.

Inviting a parent or guardian

In Settings › Parental control, the teen chooses Invite a parent and enters their email or phone number. The teen can see the settings in effect but cannot change those managed by the parent or guardian.

Invitation problems

Sign in to the personal HelpNaud account tied to the invited email or phone number, then open Settings › Parental control to accept the pending invitation. The sender can resend or cancel an invitation, or send a new one if it expired. If the problem continues, contact us (see “Contact us”) with the exact error message.

What a parent or guardian can manage

After acceptance, open Settings › Parental control and choose the teen. Planned settings are:

  • Reduce sensitive content: extra protections against age-inappropriate content.
  • Web search: allow or block web searches.
  • Image generation: allow or block image creation.
  • Voice mode and dictation.
  • Code and Co-work: allow or block code execution and access to online services from these tools.
  • Use of data to improve HelpNaud: consent to service improvement.
  • Quiet hours: time windows during which the teen cannot use HelpNaud (several windows allowed).

Turning a setting off hides or blocks the feature for the teen. Some changes apply only to new conversations. Settings are changed in Settings › Parental control, not during a conversation. HelpNaud’s other safety protections always continue to apply.

Turning off “Reduce sensitive content” does not turn a teen account into an adult account and does not remove other protections.

The teen’s privacy

Parental control does not allow reading, monitoring or exporting the teen’s conversations. HelpNaud support also cannot hand transcripts to a parent or guardian.

How HelpNaud handles personal information is described in the Privacy policy.

Safety notifications

While accounts are linked, HelpNaud may notify the parent or guardian in a limited number of situations where a serious risk to the teen’s safety is detected, for example a serious risk of self-harm, or the closing of an account following violent statements contrary to our Terms of Use.

A notification contains a short description of the type of concern and, where relevant, support resources. It contains no conversation, transcript or history.

These notifications are not real-time monitoring: they may not detect every situation and are no substitute for professional care or emergency services. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Notifications are on by default when accounts are linked and may be sent by email, SMS or in the app, depending on the contact details available. In Settings › Parental control › Safety notifications, the parent or guardian chooses how to receive them.

If you receive a notification

Check in with the teen, ask whether they are safe right now, listen without judging, and help them reach a trusted person, a professional or a crisis line.

Unlinking accounts

The teen, the parent or the guardian can choose Unlink accounts at any time. If the teen unlinks, the parent or guardian is informed. After unlinking, the parent or guardian can no longer manage settings or receive safety notifications for that account.

When the teen turns 18

When a linked account is recognized as belonging to someone aged 18 or older, parental control ends: the parent or guardian can no longer manage the account, parent notifications stop, teen-specific protections are turned off and the account holder alone controls their settings. Both people are informed. Other account settings do not change automatically.

If you are 18 or older and your account is wrongly treated as a teen account, contact us to have your age category corrected.

Parental control and trusted contact

They are two separate features. Parental control is for teens and their parent or guardian. The trusted contact is optional and for adults aged 18 or older: the chosen person may be notified if HelpNaud detects a serious suicide-related risk to their safety. That person must be at least 18. It is set up in Settings › Trusted contact.

Frequently asked questions

Can a parent or guardian read a teen’s conversations?

No, never.

Can a teen end parental control?

Yes, by unlinking the accounts; the parent or guardian is then informed.

Can accounts be linked again after parental control ends?

A parent or guardian can send a new invitation, which the account holder must accept.

Privacy Center

This page explains, in plain language, HelpNaud’s privacy settings and what they change. The full text remains the Privacy policy. Manage your choices in Settings › Privacy.

Location metadata

When this setting is on and your profile contains a region or country, HelpNaud may use this approximate location (city, region, country — never your precise GPS position) to tailor answers: taxes, local rules, currencies, times, units.

  • On by default; you can turn it off at any time.
  • When off, no location information from your profile is attached to your questions.
  • You can edit or clear it in Settings › Profile.

Help improve our future AI models

This setting is off by default. While it is off, your conversations and coding sessions are not used to train or improve HelpNaud’s future AI models.

  • If you turn it on, you allow this use going forward; you can turn it off again at any time.
  • Your choice is saved in your account and applies across your devices.
  • To request deletion of your data, write to contacts@fineciagroupe.com.

How we use your data

  • To provide the service: answer your questions, save your preferences and history, manage your plan and billing.
  • To reach you: account verification, billing, service communications; communications from FINECIA GROUPE and the HelpNaud brand may be added.
  • To understand usage: aggregated, anonymized analytics.
  • Only with your permission: improving AI models (see above).

How we protect your data

  • You stay in control of your conversation data and can change your preferences at any time.
  • We promptly delete your data on request, except in case of a violation of our safety policies or conversations you shared through feedback.
  • FINECIA GROUPE never sells your data to third parties.

Your data

  • Export data: downloads a JSON file of the preferences and data saved on this device. For a full copy of your account, write to us.
  • Shared conversations: view, copy or remove your public links.
  • Uploaded files: find them in the Library.
  • Memory preferences: Settings › Personalization.
  • Your feedback: send us feedback with the “Send feedback” button.

What these guidelines are for

The HelpNaud by FINECIA GROUPE Guidelines explain how HelpNaud takes your personal instructions into account, and what they can and cannot change. They apply to every “Instructions” field in HelpNaud (Profile, Personalization, H-Code).

How HelpNaud uses your instructions

  • Your instructions are saved in your account and attached to your questions, so they apply to your conversations on every device where you are signed in.
  • HelpNaud does its best to follow them: tone, length, level of detail, language, format, examples, field of work.
  • If two instructions conflict, the more recent or more specific one wins; HelpNaud may ask you to clarify.
  • An instruction given directly in a message takes precedence over your general instructions for that reply.

What instructions cannot do

Your instructions are preferences: they guide style and content but never change HelpNaud’s safety rules. They cannot:

  • request illegal or dangerous content, or content meant to deceive or harm others;
  • make HelpNaud pose as a real person or an official body;
  • bypass protections on teen accounts or parental-control settings;
  • ask HelpNaud to ignore privacy rules or reveal other people’s data;
  • make uncertain information look like verified fact.

Writing good instructions

  • Be specific: “short answers, formal tone, examples in euros” works better than “be good”.
  • Describe your context: job, knowledge level, tools you use.
  • Say what you would rather avoid.
  • Limit: 600 characters for HelpNaud, 500 for H-Code.

Examples

  • Short answers, formal tone, examples in euros.
  • For studying and learning concepts, arguments and analogies are especially useful to me.
  • Ask comprehension or clarifying questions before giving detailed answers.
  • I am not a beginner in programming: I mainly code in Bash, Java and Python.

Editing or removing your instructions

Open Settings › Profile (or Personalization › Custom instructions), edit the text and save. To remove them, clear the field and save.

Teen accounts

For teen accounts linked to a parent or guardian, parental-control protections always apply regardless of the instructions entered. See the Parent control policy.

General information

HelpNaud provides general information and can make mistakes. It does not replace the advice of a qualified professional (accountant, lawyer, financial adviser, doctor). Please verify important information.

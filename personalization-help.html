HelpNaud memory

Memory lets HelpNaud take into account information you ask it to keep in mind (your profession, preferences, your company’s context) to personalize its answers.

How it works today: HelpNaud only remembers items you add yourself in Settings › Personalization › Memory summary (30 items maximum, 300 characters each). It does not automatically learn from your conversations or files.

Where they are stored: in your HelpNaud account, which syncs them across your devices; you can remove them at any time. When memory is on, these items are attached to the questions sent to the AI; they are not used to train a model. See the Privacy policy.

Turn on memory

The Turn on memory switch controls whether your saved items are attached to your questions. When off, HelpNaud ignores them but they are not deleted: you can turn it back on at any time.

Avoid entering passwords, card numbers or health information.

Memory summary and saved items

Click Manage to see what HelpNaud keeps in mind. You can add an item (for example “I run a 12-person business in Montreal”), remove one, or clear everything.

Custom instructions (below) are different: they describe how HelpNaud should answer, while memory describes who you are and your context.

Custom instructions

HelpNaud: extra instructions and context (600 characters maximum) applied to all your conversations, for example “always give examples in Canadian dollars”.

H-Code: instructions (500 characters maximum) applied only in the Code tool (Interface Help), for example your preferred language or code style.

These instructions are simple preferences: they change neither HelpNaud’s safety rules nor the protections of a teen account.

Style and tone

Choose the overall tone of replies (Professional, Warm, Candid, Quirky, Efficient, Cynical), then adjust warmth, enthusiasm, emoji, visual replies and the use of headings and lists (More, Default or Less). “Default” lets HelpNaud decide.

These choices apply to your next replies and are saved in your account.

Writing: use my writing style as a reference

When on, HelpNaud adapts its writing (emails, texts, messages) to the register, length and vocabulary of your own messages in the conversation. It does not analyze your files or apps.

Reference photos

You can add a reference photo, plus body-shape, full-length and size photos, intended for future image creation of yourself and for trying on clothes and accessories.

These photos are stored only on your device and are not sent to any service. You can remove them at any time.

Web search

When on, HelpNaud runs a web search by itself when useful: current data (weather, prices, news) and, in Consult, documentary and academic research with cited sources. Each answer with a search costs 2 extra credits.

When off, HelpNaud answers without searching and may offer a “Search the web · +2 credits” button when the question requires it. The same setting exists in Settings › Capabilities.

Access recording history

When on, the transcript of each of your voice notes (microphone) is saved in your account, and HelpNaud may consult the 5 most recent ones when it answers, for example if you ask “what did I say yesterday about the accountant?”.

You can erase everything with the Erase my recordings button or turn the setting off: nothing is saved anymore. Only text is kept, never audio.

Quick answers, suggestions, voice, library and connectors

Quick answers: for a short, simple question, HelpNaud answers with a light model (1 credit). These answers are not personalized and do not use your memory. Off by default.

Prompt suggestions: a “Suggestions” button appears in the Assistant; HelpNaud proposes 3 questions based on your memory and instructions (1 credit per request).

HelpNaud Voice: turns on voice notes, voice mode and read-aloud. When off, these buttons are hidden.

Library search: before answering, HelpNaud searches your guides, templates, glossary and archived documents and relies on relevant excerpts.

Connector search: HelpNaud automatically consults official connected data sources (Bank of Canada, World Bank) for rates and economic indicators, with the source cited. Off by default.
